Who we are

Justice Society of Activism is an independent, member-funded reporting studio. We follow the paper trail — court filings, budget records, FOIA releases — and turn it into stories people can act on. No billionaire owners, no PR clients, no rage-bait. If it can't be sourced, we don't air it.

Since 2019 we've published 312 films, trained 8,400 local organizers, and handed out 1.2 million printed field guides. Our reporting has contributed to 14 policy reversals, three legislative inquiries, and an unglamorous but very real increase in the number of people who show up.

Our editorial line

We name what we can verify and we say plainly when we can't. Corrections are pinned to the video and linked in the description — not quietly deleted. If a sponsor asks us to soften a finding, we publish the finding and refund the money.
